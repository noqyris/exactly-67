Can you land on EXACTLY 67?

Exactly 67 is a chunky, satisfying number puzzle built around one deceptively simple goal: fill the right pan of a balance scale so it totals exactly 67. Hit it and the beam settles perfectly level with a happy little "six-seven" chime.

The twist is the balloons. Weights pull the pan DOWN. Balloons pull it UP. So you can overshoot 67 with a heavy weight, then pull it back down — up, really — with a balloon. That plus-and-minus tug-of-war is the whole game, and it makes every level a tiny, tactile brain teaser.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Drag weights and balloons from the tray onto the pan (or just tap to place).
- Watch the beam tip toward whichever side is heavier — the closer to 67, the closer to level.
- Land on exactly 67 to clear the level.
- No time limits. No fail states. Rearrange as much as you like. It's a calm, think-at-your-own-pace puzzle, not a reflex test.

600 LEVELS THAT KEEP CLIMBING
Twenty-five packs, each harder than the last — from a gentle opening to trays that fight back:
- Warm-Up — learn the ropes and meet the balloons.
- Prime Time — prime-heavy sets where hitting 67 gets sneaky.
- Heavy Lifting — big numbers and deep overshoots that only balloons can rescue.
- …and 22 more, all the way to The Gauntlet.
Every level is solver-verified, so there is always a way to land on 67. Along the way you'll meet locked weights and tight piece budgets that keep the ideas fresh to the end. Levels unlock one at a time as you clear them, and every level you've cleared stays open to replay.

DAILY CHALLENGE AND STREAKS
- A fresh puzzle every day, the same board for every player, ready at your own midnight.
- Solve it day after day to grow your streak. Miss a day? A streak freeze covers it — you earn them along the way.
- A monthly calendar: catch up on days you missed this month and earn a bronze, silver or gold trophy.
- Optional daily reminder, around the time you usually play. Off until you turn it on; switch it off any time in Settings.

EARN THREE STARS
Every level has a known minimum number of pieces. Solve it in the fewest weights for a perfect three-star rating. Proud of a solve? Share the route you took to 67 — it never gives the answer away.

REWARDS ALONG THE WAY
- Every new star drops into your Star Jar. A full jar pays out a free hint.
- Finish a pack to earn bonus hints and a bronze, silver or gold medal.
- A daily gift that grows each day in a row you come back: 1, 2, 4, 6, then 8 hints.

- Bold, candy-colored look with a chunky physics-toy feel.
- Gentle haptics, sound effects and a relaxed, original music track, each with its own on/off switch.

FREE TO PLAY, WITH ADS
- Free to download. The game shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and sometimes a full-screen ad between levels (never in the middle of a puzzle).
- Stuck? A hint shows you a piece that belongs in the answer. Hints come from the daily gift, the Star Jar and pack rewards, and you can watch a short optional video for extra hints and bigger rewards — always your choice.
- Optional in-app purchases: a one-time Welcome pack of 25 hints, offered until your first purchase; hint packs of 10, 30 or 100 hints; "No ads", which removes the banner and the ads between levels (hints not included; the optional videos stay); or "Unlimited hints", which gives you unlimited hints and no ads.
- You decide how your data is used for ads when the game first asks, and you can change it any time under Privacy choices on the main menu.

PLAY ANYWHERE
- The puzzles play fully offline; ads need a connection.
- No account and no sign-in. Your level progress is saved right on your device.
