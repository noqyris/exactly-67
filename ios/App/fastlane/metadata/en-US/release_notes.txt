The biggest update yet:
- 600 levels in 25 packs, up from 72, each pack harder than the last. Every level is solver-verified.
- Daily Challenge: a fresh puzzle every day, the same board for every player, ready at your own midnight.
- Daily streaks: solve the Daily Challenge day after day and watch your flame grow. Streak freezes cover a missed day, and a broken streak can still be restored for a short while after it breaks.
- A monthly calendar for the Daily Challenge: replay days you missed this month and earn bronze, silver and gold trophies.
- The Star Jar: every new star you earn fills it, and a full jar pays out a free hint.
- Pack medals and bonus hints for every pack you finish.
- A daily gift that grows each day in a row you come back: 1, 2, 4, 6, then 8 hints.
- A new Store: hint packs of 10, 30 or 100 hints, "No ads" (removes the banner and the ads between levels), and "Unlimited hints" (unlimited hints and no ads). Already removed ads before this update? You keep no ads and unlimited hints.
- Share your route to 67 from the win card, without giving the answer away.
- Relaxed background music with its own on/off switch, and a bigger celebration when you land on 67.
- Optional daily reminders, around the time you usually play. Off until you turn them on.
- A fresh main menu and win screen, a new app icon, and a layout that adapts to larger and foldable screens.
- 11 of the original 72 levels reworked into sharper "find the exact set" puzzles, and balloons now bob gently in the tray.
- Ads now come from a new ad partner. The game asks how your data may be used for ads, and you can change your answer any time under Privacy choices on the main menu.
Thanks for playing — land on exactly 67!
