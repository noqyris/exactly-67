Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Exactly 67 ("the app") is a number puzzle game. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we, the developer, run no servers: nothing you do in the game is sent to us. The app does show ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. This policy explains what, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

Unless you have bought an ad-free option (see In-app purchases), the app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and short videos you can choose to watch to earn a hint.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource), with ads from the Unity Ads network. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your consent and, for the advertising identifier, your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.

Your choices

The ad software does not start as soon as you open the app. After the opening animation, the app first asks for your permission:

  • Tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • "Ads and your data". An in-app consent prompt asks whether our ad partners may use your device's advertising identifier and information about the ads you see to personalise those ads. If you tap Accept, the ad software starts. If you tap Decline, the ad software is not started at all, so it collects nothing and no ads are shown, including the optional hint videos. Every level still plays.

If you accept the consent prompt but decline tracking, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not used to personalise them.

To change your consent later, tap Privacy choices in the top corner of the main menu. It opens the same prompt again. If you withdraw consent after ads have already started, the app removes the banner at once, stops showing full-screen ads and hint videos, stops loading new ads, and tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. The ad software itself stays loaded until the app is closed, and from the next start it is not started at all. The link is not shown if you own Unlimited hints, because the ad software never runs for you.

Many Unity ads also show a Data Privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's Cookie & Advertising Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional:

  • Hint packs of 10, 30 or 100 hints. Each pack can be bought as many times as you like, and bought hints never expire. Your hint balance is stored on your device, so it is lost if you delete the app.
  • No ads, a one-time purchase. It removes the banner and the full-screen ads between levels. Hints are not included. The hint videos stay available if you choose to watch one, so the ad software still runs (if you have given consent).
  • Unlimited hints, a one-time purchase. It gives you unlimited hints and no ads. With it, the app does not start the ad software at all.

Purchases are processed by Apple (on Android, by Google Play). The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device. Restore purchases in the Store asks Apple to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, using the operating system's standard app storage:

  • Your level progress (stars earned and the fewest pieces you used per level) and the date you last finished the Daily Challenge.
  • Your hint balance and the date of your last free daily hint.
  • Your sound, music and haptics settings.
  • Which purchases you own.
  • A count of recent level clears, used to space out full-screen ads, and whether the app has already asked you to rate it.
  • Your ad consent choice, which the ad software stores so it isn't asked again every launch, and a flag recording that the app has already cleared, once, any consent answer left over from an earlier version of the app.

None of this is sent to us. It is removed if you delete the app.

Ratings and sharing

After you clear a level, the app may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt, once. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave. The Share button on the win card opens your device's share sheet with a short text summary of how you solved the level. It contains no personal data, and where you send it is up to you.

Network

The puzzles themselves work fully offline. The app uses the network only to load ads (after you have given consent), to make or restore purchases through Apple, and for Apple's rating prompt.

Children

Exactly 67 is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the ads in this app, contact us at the address below.
